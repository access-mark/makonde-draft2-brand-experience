Privacy

How this draft handles enquiries

The contact form prepares an email in your own email application. No form submission is stored by this static website. If you send the email, your email provider and Makonde will process the information contained in your message.

Before public launch, Makonde must approve a complete POPIA-aligned privacy notice covering the responsible party, purposes, lawful basis, retention, data subject rights, service providers and contact routes.

Website enquiry processing

When you submit the contact form, Makonde receives the details you provide, including your name, organisation, email address, area of interest and message, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

The form uses FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Your submitted information is transmitted to FormSubmit for delivery to Makonde’s designated mailbox. Do not submit passwords, identity numbers or other sensitive information.

The form does not subscribe you to marketing communications. Retention periods, third-party processing terms and the final privacy notice must be confirmed by Makonde before public launch.
